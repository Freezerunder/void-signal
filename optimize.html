✅ How this works

Three rules, so you can trust the buttons.

1

Nothing is hosted here

Straight to the source

Every download button points at the developer's own release page or site — GitHub Releases, or the vendor's download page. No mirrors, no repackaged installers, no bundled extras. That means you always get the current version, signed by whoever actually made it.

2

Links point at “latest”

They don't rot

For anything on GitHub the link goes to /releases/latest rather than a pinned file. When the developer ships an update, this page is already pointing at it — there's no stale version number to go out of date.

3

The previews are drawings

Not screenshots

Each card shows a hand-drawn diagram of what that app actually puts on your screen — the notch strip, the charge gauge, the search results list. They're stylised on purpose, so nothing here pretends to be a real screenshot of software it isn't.
